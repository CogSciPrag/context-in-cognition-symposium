The symposium will take place at the building at [Keplerstrasse 2](https://maps.app.goo.gl/hR616sMiLk9TBM4BA) in Tübingen. The main entrance is from the Keplerstrasse, and the symposium will take place in the rooms 001 -- 004. THey are located on the first floor right next to the main entrance. There will be signs indicating the rooms, so no need to worry. 

The keynotes, the workshops and the contributed talks will take place in room 001. The poster sessions will take place in rooms 003 and 004. The coffee breaks will be provided in room 002.
